# Action game research: root partition

Read on 2026-10-13. 132 accepted author game pages, 27 rejected after reading, 19 HTTP/description failures, 2 unread valid candidates. These are page studies, not playthroughs. HTTP responses were decoded explicitly as UTF-8 after isolating the incorrect ISO-8859-1 fallback. `verified.jsonl` includes individual authored mechanic notes and content hashes. Description excerpts are at most 25 words; full fetched descriptions are local working evidence, not publication content.

The reading scope is a breadth pass over the main author description (typically the first 1,100–1,250 characters) and platform metadata, with additional segments for several long pages. It does not establish that every advertised feature exists, is balanced or performs well. Counts from marketing copy remain attributed claims. No final game design is selected in this document.

## Samples 1–50: decisions during fighting

- [You Are Circle](https://ltjax.itch.io/you-are-circle) describes a concrete chain: movement-stun, pull, then area impact. This is stronger evidence of a distinct play style than a count of upgrades.
- [Room Cleaner](https://spals.itch.io/room-cleaner) describes one-hit combat plus windows, barrels and conveyors. Short encounters can ask for spatial decisions, but the author page does not prove the result feels fair.
- [Mr. Marshmallow Man](https://fartfish.itch.io/mr-marshmallow-man) has enemies shoot back when damaged; choosing when to attack creates danger. Its upgrade list changes triggers (dash/hurt), not just damage.
- [Karashuta](https://s4g.itch.io/karashuta) makes colour switching alter both defense and offense, and enemy absorption means the same rule applies to both sides.
- [Rogue Android](https://michael-darling.itch.io/rogue-android) uses kill energy to activate deck-built abilities. Its cancellation status matters: mechanics are documented but ongoing maintenance is not claimed.
- [Super Pixel Robot Rampage](https://firsttry.itch.io/sprr) explicitly says its menu does not pause. [Playin' With Fire](https://officialchaser8.itch.io/playin-with-fire) warns of stacking/restart bugs. These are useful failure cases, not quality endorsements.

## Samples 51–100: variety, pacing and portability

- [100 Bullet Dungeon](https://thoof.itch.io/100-bullet-dungeon) ties attacks to recoverable ammunition, then resets ammunition on room clear. That creates an in-room cost without post-room cleanup.
- [Auto Mech Dungeon](https://dapssenpai.itch.io/auto-mech-dungeon) reloads through dodging; [XENOFLEKT](https://catoio-software.itch.io/xenoflekt) heals only through successful deflection. Both link movement to a resource instead of adding another action button.
- [Umberang](https://kristoff-red.itch.io/umberang) has a shop every four rooms but openly lacks pause/settings/dash. A small content count does not excuse incomplete core controls.
- [Looploop](https://certifiedbozo.itch.io/looploop) specifies the Boss in Room 8. [Disfigure: Prologue](https://coldbrewentertainment.itch.io/disfigure-prologue) specifies ten-minute runs and quick restarts. These establish legible completion targets; they do not prove our required pace without playtesting.
- [Pixel Wizard Arena](https://chillu.itch.io/pixelwizardarena) offers optional automatic aiming. [OctoMech Survivors](https://arkhamsoft.itch.io/octomech-survivors) combines auto-fire with weapon weight and active repairs. Automatic attack need not remove all player choices, but it is different from the user's Isaac reference.
- [Further Still](https://arcaneinput.itch.io/further-still-survivors), [Planetary Exploration Company](https://chrisibarra.itch.io/pec), and [Lost Souls](https://averagepiglet-atroxit.itch.io/lost-souls) state browser save/audio/render limitations. Do not infer browser reliability from an HTML5 badge.

## Samples 101–132: short-room progression and authored variation

- [Krypta](https://jkostkaa.itch.io/krypta) describes two-item treasure choices, health-trading altars and bosses that change below half health. The page discloses AI-created code/art/music; it is gameplay research only and not an art source for this user's download preference.
- [DANMAKU GLADIATOR](https://kanzaki-shintarou.itch.io/danmaku-gladiator-champions) selects a random item between consecutive boss fights: a clear combat/reward cadence without a large exploration layer.
- [Dungeon Catering](https://alexortiz.itch.io/dungeon-catering) connects enemy drops to weapon recipes. [DiceDungeon](https://barret232hxc.itch.io/dicedungeon) documents stackable drones and corpse explosions rather than only generic stats.
- [Dreadrun](https://armanixboi.itch.io/dreadrun) splits finding gear from protecting a payload; objectives can change the value of a build.
- [Nebulous Infinity](https://virtually-competent.itch.io/nebulous-infinity) makes crates an open-versus-sell choice, and unidentified powerups may be harmful. Risk must be intelligible if runs are meant to feel immediately approachable.

## Open questions for direct play

How long is the first useful interaction? Can a new player distinguish enemy shot, friendly shot and loot? Are movement/aim responsive? Does damage feel avoidable? Does a selected upgrade visibly change the next fight? Can the player pause, restart and return after refresh? These require browser interaction; source descriptions alone cannot answer them.

Official reference pages were also read through web browsing (outside the 132 count): [The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth](https://store.steampowered.com/app/250900/The_Binding_of_Isaac_Rebirth/) and [Enter the Gungeon](https://store.steampowered.com/app/311690/Enter_the_Gungeon/). The former emphasizes transformation through discovered items and seeded runs; the latter explicitly describes authored rooms inside a procedural labyrinth, dodging and environmental cover. Neither page is permission to reuse its characters or audiovisual assets.
