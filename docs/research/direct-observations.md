## Room Cleaner — first tutorial room and upgrade selection

Author page: https://spals.itch.io/room-cleaner

Ran the browser game, used the fullscreen button, then Space on the title screen. Actually moved using W/A/S/D, kicked the first door using right mouse, saw the guard knocked down, approached and picked up a dropped pistol, walked through the now-open FIGHT door, and chose the middle upgrade using the 2 key.

Observed: movement tutorial changes its prompt once performed; the door highlights with a right-mouse cue; the weapon HUD switches from a dash to 12 rounds when a pistol is acquired; room completion changes the instruction to PICK A DOOR. Three upgrade cards appeared with short effects (same-gun ammunition bonus, faster fire, larger magazine). Selection displays the chosen card before returning to combat. The first tutorial room has unlimited hearts; this session therefore **does not establish normal combat difficulty, death/restart behavior, or late-run balance**.

Practical observation: contextual highlighting made the next operation legible, but right mouse is used for kicking, picking up and throwing according to context. My initial pickup attempt acted on a different contextual target. Any similarly overloaded input needs a clear target cue. Fullscreen settled into the expected complete viewport after resizing; initial transitional captures were smaller and do not establish a persistent scaling defect.

## 100 Bullet Dungeon — tutorial movement, first room entry and failure return

Author page: https://thoof.itch.io/100-bullet-dungeon

Launched the Unity browser game from its author page. Fractional iframe input coordinates prevented reliable embedded interaction in the first attempt; opened the exact observed public iframe URL instead. Clicked Play and pressed Space through the control screen, moved upward from the tutorial room through the door into the first enclosed room, and saw a slime pursuing the player. While inspecting other research notes the simulation continued and the player died. Pressing Space returned to the title.

This confirms launch, movement, room transition and the displayed failure-return flow only. It does not establish shooting, normal survivability, clearing, upgrades or completion; the unattended death is not evidence that the game is unfair. In the observed 882px browser viewport the fixed-width embedded canvas extended off the right edge with a horizontal scrollbar. That is a limitation of this direct embedded view, not a claim about every viewport or the author page's fullscreen behavior.
